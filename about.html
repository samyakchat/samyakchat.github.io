My Journey

Here is a timeline of the milestones and projects that have shaped my skills so far. Note, between these projects are many mini projects I used to learn along the way.

2021

I started coding around 5th grade learning Python.

2023

Python seemed to stick and in 7th grade I made LifeLine. Around this time I started to investigate AI.

2023 - 2024

I made a few smaller undocumented projects, such as a mini help screen for my MacBook that allowed touchscreen control via a Python program.

2023

Once my Python skills were strong, I entered a coding competition and won 3rd place competing against multiple states.

2024 - 2025

Competed in FBLA for coding and programming and created Money-Mate, where I first learned to incorporate AI.

2025

Present to 2026
